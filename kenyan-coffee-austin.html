Kenyan coffee in Austin, Texas

Single-origin beans, Kifaru cold brew and a mobile coffee cart, from a Kenyan-rooted roaster in Austin.

The short answer: Kahoa Coffee is an Austin-based roaster of single-origin Kenyan coffee. You can order beans online with free local delivery, and book our cold brew and mobile coffee cart for weddings, offices and events.

Kenyan coffee, roasted in Texas

Kahoa imports single-origin coffee from highland farms in Kenya and has it roasted in Texas in small batches. Every bag is 340g and is available as whole bean or ground. Free local delivery is applied automatically at checkout for Austin-area addresses, so there is no code to enter.

Kifaru cold brew

Kifaru is Kahoa's cold brew, made from our Kenyan coffee. Choose Kifaru Classic, smooth and original, or Kifaru Spice, flavored with vanilla bean, ginger, cardamom, clove and cinnamon the way Kenya takes its chai. Both are available as still cold brew or Nitro (Draft).

A coffee cart for your Austin event

Our mobile coffee cart brings an espresso bar, Kifaru cold brew and Kenyan single-origin coffee to weddings, offices, launches and private events around Austin, Manor, Round Rock and the Hill Country. Packages start at $650, and you can choose an espresso bar, a hand-poured Pour-Over Reserve, or both. See the coffee cart page or book online.

Wholesale for Austin cafes and restaurants

Run a cafe, restaurant or office? Kahoa supplies single-origin Kenyan coffee to local businesses. See the wholesale page or email info@kahoa.shop.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Kenyan coffee in Austin?

You can order Kahoa's single-origin Kenyan coffee online at kahoa.shop, with free local delivery to Austin-area addresses applied automatically at checkout.

Does Kahoa deliver in Austin?

Yes. Local Austin-area delivery is free and is applied automatically at checkout. Orders to other addresses ship by standard shipping.

Does Kahoa offer a coffee cart for weddings in Austin?

Yes. Kahoa's mobile coffee cart serves weddings, offices and private events around Austin with an espresso bar, Kifaru cold brew, and an optional hand-poured Pour-Over Reserve. Packages start at $650.

What is Kifaru cold brew?

Kifaru is Kahoa's cold brew made from single-origin Kenyan coffee, in Classic and Spice flavors, served cold or as Nitro (Draft).

Does Kahoa sell wholesale coffee in Austin?

Yes. Kahoa supplies single-origin Kenyan coffee to local cafes, restaurants and offices. Contact info@kahoa.shop.
