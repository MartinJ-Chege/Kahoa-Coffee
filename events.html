Questions

Do you serve coffee during SXSW, ACL and Formula 1 weeks in Austin?

Yes. We take brand and corporate bookings across the Austin area, including during major-event weeks. Peak dates are limited, so we recommend inquiring as early as you can.

Is Kahoa Coffee affiliated with SXSW, Austin City Limits or Formula 1?

No. Kahoa Coffee is an independent Austin coffee company. We serve private brand and corporate events held around these weeks, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by SXSW, Austin City Limits or Formula 1.

What can you serve at an activation or hospitality event?

Barista-made espresso drinks, a pour-over reserve, and Kifaru Spice Cold Brew and Spice Nitro on tap. Our cart has two taps, and every drink is made with our single-origin Kenyan coffee.

Are you insured for on-site events?

Yes. Kahoa is fully insured for on-site event service. Tell us about your venue and we will walk through what it requires.

Can you tailor the menu to our brand?

Yes. Custom cups, signage and menu cards are part of our Signature Brand Activation and Flagship Festival Takeover packages. We also create branded signature drinks and latte art around your brand.

How much does an event cost?

Every event is quoted individually based on headcount, hours, menu and date. Our coffee cart page shows package starting points, and we send a custom quote within 1 business day of your request.

Kahoa Coffee is an independent Austin company. SXSW, Austin City Limits and Formula 1 are the property of their respective owners. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by them, and we serve private brand and corporate events held around these weeks.
