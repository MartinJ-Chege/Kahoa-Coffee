Agreement

1. Services

Kahoa Coffee will provide the package, add-ons, staffing, and equipment shown in your booking summary, on the date, start time, and location listed. Hours of service and staffing follow the package you selected.

2. Payment

A 30% deposit is due at booking to hold your date, with the balance due 7 days before your event. Paying in full at booking earns a 5% discount on the service portion.

Custom branding items (logo stickers, full custom-printed cups) are billed in full at booking, separate from and in addition to the deposit, since they are produced specifically for your event and cannot be reused. Full Custom-Printed Cups require 21 business days' lead time; Logo Stickers require 10 business days.

3. Add-Ons

Event DJ services are provided by our partner DJ and confirmed within 24 hours of booking.

4. Overtime & Overage

Service beyond the hours included in your package is billed at $140 per additional hour, charged after the event. Guest counts that exceed your reported headcount by more than a 15% buffer are billed at $7 per additional guest served. By checking the authorization box at payment, you allow Kahoa Coffee to charge the card used at booking for these fees if they are incurred.

5. Guest Count

Final guest count is due 7 days before your event. Increases after that point may incur additional staffing or product costs, quoted separately.

6. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Deposits are non-refundable. Cancelling more than 30 days before your event forfeits the deposit only. Cancelling within 30 days incurs 50% of the remaining balance; within 7 days or a no-show incurs the full balance. One free reschedule is available with 14+ days' notice, subject to availability. Branding items already in production are non-refundable regardless of timing.

7. Venue & Setup

Client is responsible for providing a suitable, level setup space with access to a standard power outlet within 25 ft, and any permits required by the venue or city. Kahoa Coffee may adjust or pause outdoor service for severe weather or safety, with no refund for time missed due to conditions outside our control.

8. Liability

Kahoa Coffee carries general liability insurance for on-site event service.

1. Products & Delivery

Kahoa Coffee will supply the 5-gallon cold brew kegs in your order, delivered ready to tap to the service location above, on the delivery schedule you selected (one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly). Delivery is included for Austin-area addresses.

2. Payment

Your first order is charged in full by card at checkout at the VIP price shown, plus a refundable keg deposit of $50 per keg (see Kegs below). For recurring schedules, Kahoa Coffee will email you an invoice or payment link before each delivery at the price in your order, payable by card. Payment is due before the delivery, and we will tell you before any price change.

3. Kegs

Empty kegs remain the property of Kahoa Coffee and are collected or swapped at your next delivery, or by arrangement for one-time orders. A refundable deposit of $50 per keg is charged on your first order only, not on later deliveries, and is refunded to your card when the empty kegs are returned in good condition. Lost or damaged kegs are billed at replacement cost, less any deposit held. Please keep kegs refrigerated after delivery; Kahoa Coffee is not responsible for quality loss from improper storage.

4. Kegerator Lease

The kegerator lease is $50 per month, billed monthly by card starting the day you sign up. The lease is waived once you are on a standing order of 2+ kegs per month (email us and we'll cancel the lease charge). A refundable equipment deposit of $200 applies, which we will invoice by email either as one payment or, if you choose, as two payments of $100 (the first when the lease starts and the second one month later), and return when the unit is returned in good condition. The deposit is waived once you are on a standing order of 2+ kegs per month. The kegerator remains the property of Kahoa Coffee. You are responsible for it while it is at your location; damage beyond normal wear or loss is billed at replacement cost. Provide a level spot with a standard power outlet, and do not move or modify the unit without telling us. Either of us may end the lease with 30 days' notice (email is fine), and you'll make the unit accessible for pickup.

5. Location & Access

You confirm that the service location can receive deliveries and that someone will be available at delivery, or that you have arranged access in advance. We may reschedule a delivery if access is not available.

6. Changes & Cancellation

One-time orders can be changed or cancelled up to 48 hours before the scheduled delivery. Standing delivery schedules can be paused or cancelled with 7 days' notice (email is fine). Orders already delivered are non-refundable.

7. Liability

Kahoa Coffee carries general liability insurance. You are responsible for the safe storage, tapping, and serving of the product once it is delivered.

This is a digital operating agreement between you and Kahoa Coffee to guide our booking and service process — it is not intended as a substitute for a lawyer-drafted contract.
